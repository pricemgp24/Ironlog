Rotational Core — Waist Reduction
OBLIQUE · ROTATION
Cable Woodchop High-to-Low 🏆
Cable high, pull diagonally across body to opposite hip. Thoracic rotation only — not lower back. 12 reps each side. This builds the obliques and tightens the waist.
OBLIQUE · ANTI-ROT
Pallof Press + Rotation 🏆
Cable at chest, stand sideways, press out, hold 2 sec fighting rotation, add slow 45° turn away from cable. 10 each side. Builds the bracing strength that holds your waist tight under load.
CORE · CRUNCH
Cable Twisting Crunch 🏆
Kneel facing away, rope at head. Crunch AND rotate — elbow to opposite knee. Slow. 15 each side. Direct oblique work + crunch = waist reduction.

85lb sandbag · 60 sec rest between · Time each carry
Bear hug grip — bag against chest. Short fast steps, tall posture. Target under 18 sec per 50 yards. Don't set the bag down during a carry — finishing matters more than speed.

6-WEEK EQUATION

HTWRDaily Zone 2 burns fat. Oblique work tightens waist. Caloric deficit drops the inches. 30 days to Aug 31.
DL PEAKTue volume + Fri heavy. Progressive from 70% → 95% across 4 weeks then maintain.
2-MILETue tempo · Wed VO2 max · Fri fartlek. Three different run stimuli per week covering the full Galpin aerobic spectrum.
CARRYFri sandbag protocol escalates from 6×50yd at 85lbs to 8×50yd at 95lbs then tapers. Real load on real turf.
SIZEMon shoulders+arms · Wed chest+tri · Thu back+bi. Every upper body group hit with hypertrophy volume.
LEGSTue lower hypertrophy. Fri lower power. Sat hike. Three different leg stimuli, no back-to-back days.
COREMon rotational core — woodchops, Pallof, twisting crunch. Direct oblique work for waist reduction and carry stability.

NUTRITION FOR WAIST REDUCTION

DEFICITDrop 300–500 cal/day from maintenance. At your training volume that's about 2,500–2,700 cal/day. Protein stays at 1g/lb — 185g minimum daily. Never cut protein.
CARBSMore on Tue/Wed/Fri (hard days). Less on Mon/Thu/Sat. Cycle carbs around the workout window.
WATERHalf bodyweight in oz + 16oz extra per training hour. Hydration directly affects waist measurement — dehydration causes water retention around the midsection.
TIMINGStop eating 3 hours before bed. Morning fasted cardio is not required — the caloric deficit is what matters, not when you eat.
