NUTRITION — 235P / 365C / 75F

MACROS3,095 cal/day. At 0.5% BF cut/week you need ~350 cal deficit on rest days. Training days eat at maintenance or slight surplus to fuel sessions. Carb cycle: higher carbs Tue/Wed/Fri (hard days), lower Mon/Thu/Sat.
PRE-GYMBanana + uncrustable = ~45g carbs, ~8g protein. Enough for the session. Post-workout within 30 min: 40g protein shake minimum. This is non-negotiable on TRT — the anabolic window is real when androgens are elevated.

SLEEPCasein + cottage cheese every night. 8–9 hours. On TRT your recovery capacity is higher but only if sleep is protected.
