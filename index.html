Hex Bar Deadlift — AFT Primary
DEADLIFT · AFT EVENT
Hex Bar Deadlift — Volume 🏆 PR
70% = ~220lbs · 80% = ~250lbs · 90% = ~285lbs. Full reset between reps — competition style not touch-and-go. Stand tall at lockout every rep.
⚑ AFT StandardAFT tests 3 reps max. Volume at 70–80% builds the base. Peak weeks push near ceiling so 3 reps on test day feels submaximal.
Knee-Safe Lower Body
QUAD · PRIMARY
Leg Press Machine 🏆 PR
Shallow ROM — feet shoulder width, mid-platform. Primary quad builder. Go heavy — leg press load is knee-safe unlike axial squat load.
Galpin — HypertrophyHigh rep ranges in a stretched position drive maximal hypertrophic signaling. Leg press allows full quad loading without spinal compression.
QUAD · ISOLATION
Leg Extension 🏆 PR
2-second squeeze at peak contraction. Controlled descent. Superset with seated leg curl in weeks 4–6 to save time.
HAMSTRING
Seated Leg Curl 🏆 PR
3-second slow negative every rep. Hamstrings are the prime mover in the hex bar deadlift. Stronger hamstrings = bigger pull. This directly transfers to AFT score.
GLUTE · CARRY POWER
Hip Thrust — Barbell 🏆 PR
Squeeze and hold 1 sec at top. Strong glutes = faster runs and heavier pulls.
⚑ AFT CarryLoaded carry demands glute stability on every step. Weak glutes = lateral sway under load = slower time.

Power + Carry
POWER · EXPLOSIVE
Box Jump — Max Intent 🏆 PR
Full reset between reps. Land softly. Max intent every rep — jump height is the stimulus, not the load.
Galpin — Power DomainPower = force × velocity. Explosive intent at moderate load improves rate of force development — directly improves running speed and deadlift bar speed.
CARRY · AFT EVENT
Farmer Carry — 40lb DBs 🏆 PR
Log height as carry weight (40 or 50). Log reps as time in seconds. Tall posture, tight core, short fast steps. Target under 20 sec per 40 yards.
⚑ AFT CarryWks 5–8 carry 50lbs each hand. Makes 40lbs feel easy on test day.
POSTERIOR CHAIN
Romanian Deadlift — DB 🏆 PR
Heaviest DBs available. Hip hinge, feel the hamstring stretch, drive hips forward. Posterior chain carries your deadlift and your run.

Hamstrings — Primary Focus
HAM · LYING CURL
Lying Leg Curl Machine 🏆 PR
Prone position hits hamstrings differently than seated — you're training both heads across the week. 3-second slow negative every rep. Full extension at bottom before curling up. This is your second hamstring session of the week — Monday was seated, today is lying.
Galpin — FrequencyHitting hamstrings twice per week with different machines (seated vs lying) trains the muscle through different joint angles. More total weekly volume = more hypertrophic stimulus = more size.
HAM · RDL
Romanian Deadlift — DB 🏆 PR
Heaviest DBs available. Hip hinge, deep stretch at bottom, drive hips forward. Saturday RDL is heavier than Thursday since Thursday's RDL follows heavy deadlift — today you have fresh legs. Push the weight here.
⚑ AFT TransferPosterior chain strength carries your 2-mile and your deadlift. Two RDL sessions per week is one of the highest-leverage things in this program.
Glutes — Full Development
GLUTE · PRIMARY
Hip Thrust — Barbell 🏆 PR
Same as Monday but go heavier — you're fresh today. Squeeze and hold 1 second at full hip extension every rep. This is the single best glute exercise that exists. Push the weight up every week you can.
GLUTE · ABDUCTOR
Seated Hip Abduction Machine 🏆 PR
Push out slowly, control the return. Outer glute and hip abductor. Monday doesn't hit this — Saturday is the only abductor work in the week. Hip abductor strength stabilizes your stride under fatigue — critical for the final 800m of your 2-mile.
GLUTE · CABLE
Cable Pull-Through 🏆 PR
Rope between legs, face away from cable. Hip hinge forward then drive hips forward hard — squeeze glutes at top. Pure hip hinge pattern, back completely safe. Trains the same movement as the top of your deadlift lockout.
Quad Finisher + Calves
QUAD · FINISHER
Leg Press — Feet High and Wide 🏆 PR
Feet high and wide on the platform shifts emphasis to glutes and hamstrings vs Monday's standard position. Different foot placement = different stimulus. After all the posterior chain work these will burn hard — that's the point. 3 sets, moderate weight, high reps.
CALVES
Standing Calf Raise — Toes Out / Toes In 🏆 PR
Alternate toe angles each set — out, in, out, in. Full range every rep — all the way down to stretch, all the way up to peak contraction, hold 1 second. Calf endurance directly affects your 2-mile form in the final mile — when calves fatigue, your stride shortens.
⚑ AFT TransferStrong calves = maintained stride length under fatigue. Log the weight as machine weight and reps — track both toe positions as separate entries.

THE GALPIN EQUATION — YOUR 8 WEEKS

STRENGTHMon + Thu hex bar work. Progressive overload — 315 to 365+ is achievable with this protocol.
POWERThu box jumps + carry sprint. Rate of force development = deadlift bar speed + running power.
HYPERTLegs twice per week (Mon + Sat). Upper body twice per week (Tue + Fri). Every muscle group gets 2x frequency — the threshold for maximum muscle growth.
VO2 MAXWed weeks 5–6. Highest return investment for 2-mile time drop. Don't skip these.
ANAERSaturday carry on fatigued legs trains the energy system under load — simulates the AFT event sequence.
ZONE 2Wednesday throughout. The aerobic base everything else sits on.
SKILLLoaded carry post-lift every Saturday. Carry under fatigue = AFT-specific adaptation.
